# Voice Profile & Aesthetic Guidelines
- Tone is grounded, observant, and visually vivid.
- Prioritize internal rhymes and consonant matches over simple end-couplets.
- Delivery pace favors 10 to 12 syllables per bar with deliberate pauses.
- Avoid cliché party rap tropes; anchor storytelling in real sensory details (rain on asphalt, faint neon, old cassette hiss).
