# Creative Rules & Negative Constraints

## Writing Rules
- Never use filler phrases like "check it out", "you know what I'm sayin'", or "listen up" unless deliberately marked as an adlib.
- Maintain consistent metaphor continuity: if a verse opens with navigation/ocean imagery, sustain water or celestial navigation motifs.
- Enforce strict syllable count variance: adjacent bars in a couplet should differ by at most ±1 syllable for tight pocket lock.

## Prohibited Clichés
- Avoid rhyming "fire" with "desire".
- Avoid rhyming "pain" with "rain" unless subverted with internal multi-syllable rhyme schemes.
- Avoid generic brag lines without specific situational imagery.
